# Answer results

Generated by `make tables` from the run logs in `runs/generation/`; don't edit by hand.

Each cell is the mean with its 95% interval: Wilson for shares of questions, a bootstrap over questions for the others. Rules in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("how an answer run is scored"). **Correct**: the answer passed the check and states every key fact, over the answerable questions with key facts. On the test split, the owner's hand grades add the answerable questions without key facts (`runs/grades/hand-grades.v1.jsonl`, #88): an answer graded correct is correct, one graded partly correct or wrong is not, nor is a refusal, an answer that failed the check or a missing reply. **Citation precision**: the share of an answer's cited passages that are, or lie inside, a gold passage, over the answerable questions answered. **Citation recall**: the share of gold passages cited, over every answerable question. **Refusal accuracy**: unanswerable questions refused. **False refusals**: answerable questions refused. **Not answered reliably**: the reply failed the check, so the reader was told it could not be answered reliably. **Faithful**: the share of answers in which every claim follows from the passages they cite, as judged by claude-opus-5-5 with instructions `b6b94f34888a`, the judge chosen in #83, over the answers it gave a usable verdict on; only answers that passed the check and aren't refusals are judged. *Not judged* means the judge hasn't been run on that run's answers. Cost, seconds and tokens are per question with a reply; a run sent through the batch service paid half price and has no seconds, as its replies have no time of their own. Words shown is the length of the text of the passages put in each prompt, headings included, counted as in the retrieval results; the input tokens also count the instructions and the passages' markup.
