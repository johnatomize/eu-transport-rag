# Answer results

Generated by `make tables` from the run logs in `runs/generation/`; don't edit by hand.

Each cell is the mean with its 95% interval: Wilson for shares of questions, a bootstrap over questions for the others. Rules in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("how an answer run is scored"). **Correct**: the answer passed the check and states every key fact, over the answerable questions with key facts. **Citation precision**: the share of an answer's cited passages that are, or lie inside, a gold passage, over the answerable questions answered. **Citation recall**: the share of gold passages cited, over every answerable question. **Refusal accuracy**: unanswerable questions refused. **False refusals**: answerable questions refused. **Not answered reliably**: the reply failed the check, so the reader was told it could not be answered reliably. Cost, seconds and tokens are per question with a reply; a run sent through the batch service paid half price and has no seconds, as its replies have no time of their own. Words shown is the length of the text of the passages put in each prompt, headings included, counted as in the retrieval results; the input tokens also count the instructions and the passages' markup.
