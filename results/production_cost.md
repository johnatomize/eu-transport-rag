## Lever: caching the fixed instructions

Estimated, not measured. Every answer prompt starts with the same instructions and answer schema, about 1,126–1,149 tokens, which can be cached; the passages and the question change every time. Prices, claude-opus-5-5: a hit costs US$0.20 per million tokens instead of US$4.00, and a miss writes the cache at US$5.00 (5 minutes) or US$8.00 (1 hour). Anthropic's documentation doesn't say whether the instructions the API adds for the answer schema are part of the cached prefix; if they aren't, a little over half as many tokens are cached and the savings shrink in proportion. The share of hits assumes questions come at random times at an even rate; real traffic bunches in working hours, which raises it. The reranker's prompts are left out: they cost too little for caching to matter.
